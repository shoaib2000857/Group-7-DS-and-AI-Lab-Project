6 · Outside LEVIR

On new places, nothing beats the simple rule

Everything above is one dataset of Texas towns. So we ran the same systems on 100 pairs from each of two others, scored the same way: WHU-CDC, aerial photos of Christchurch, New Zealand, and SECOND-CC, satellite photos of several Chinese cities. Neither marks roads, so only building claims are checked there.

RSCaMa's lead disappears. On WHU-CDC it described new buildings on — of — unchanged pairs; in one we checked by eye, the same warehouse stood in both photos and only its yard had changed. On SECOND-CC it missed — of — real building changes. Our retrained copy, a tie at home, did worse away. Qwen3-VL held up best of the learned models on SECOND-CC, and the pixel template, with no learning at all, was within noise of the best on both.

7 · So what

What this means for TerraWatch

  • Check captions claim by claim. One overlap score hid a model inventing changes on most unchanged scenes.
  • Don't trust a captioner off its home turf. The best model on LEVIR fell to the level of a no-learning rule on new places. Evidence from the pixels, and a check of every claim against it, matter more than which captioner we pick. Building that check is the next stage.
  • Specialist as baseline, general model as challenger. Qwen3-VL writes richer descriptions and held up best on SECOND-CC, but needs that check before anyone relies on it.
  • On LEVIR, the specialist's lead is real. Retrained so it never saw the test pairs, RSCaMa matched its released score with a fifth of the training data.
  • Still to do: a blinded human audit, the claim checker, and a final run on — sealed pairs.

How these numbers were made

  • Both general models got the same instruction; RSCaMa writes its own style of caption. A rule-based parser splits each caption into claims. A claim checks out when the mask shows that kind of change and the reference captions agree on its direction.
  • In — of the — pairs we opened, the mask and the captions disagree (for example, a road marked changed because the photos shifted slightly). Those pairs are left out of the rates.
  • Every number was re-derived independently from the raw outputs. An AI-assisted spot check of — claims found the parser faithful on —, and the verdict right on — of the — it could judge. It is not yet a human audit.
