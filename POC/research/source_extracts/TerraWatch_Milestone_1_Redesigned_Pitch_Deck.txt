04 / DELIBERATE FOCUS
Start narrow. Make claims auditable.
Milestone 1 centers on high-resolution built-up change rather than generic Earth monitoring.
IN SCOPE
✓
Co-registered RGB image pairs (~0.5 m/pixel)
✓
Buildings, roads and groundwork in built-up areas
✓
Pixel-level mask and grounded short caption
✓
Lighting / blur / alignment robustness tests
✓
Public benchmarks, human audit and local demo
OUT OF SCOPE
Legal status / ownership / intent
Causal explanations of why
Damage or monetary estimates
Unvalidated Sentinel-2 use
Nationwide real-time deployment

08 / THE GAP
What if the sentence could be checked?
The novelty claim is evidence grounding and evaluation - not inventing a new detector or pairing two models.
ILLUSTRATIVE CHANGE EVIDENCE
UNVERIFIED GENERATION
“Seven buildings were added after illegal clearing.”
CHECK INDIVIDUAL CLAIMS
Object class   •   Count   •   Relative location   •   Unsupported inference
“Buildings appear in the central portion of the image.”
Example wording is illustrative and does not describe an evaluated model output.
